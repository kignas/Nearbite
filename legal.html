Terms of Service

The rules for using Nearbite

Using Nearbite

Nearbite is a technology platform that lets you browse menus and place food orders with independent restaurant partners. By using Nearbite, you agree to these Terms and to our Privacy Policy.

Your account

You need an account, verified by a one-time code sent to your phone number, to place an order. You're responsible for keeping your account secure and for all activity under it. Let us know right away if you suspect unauthorized access.

Accurate delivery information

You agree to provide an accurate delivery address and contact number. Nearbite and our delivery partners aren't responsible for delays or failed deliveries caused by incorrect or incomplete information you provide.

Restaurant partners & menus

Restaurants on Nearbite are independent businesses, not Nearbite employees or agents. They set their own menus, prices, and availability, which can change without notice. Responsibility for the quality, safety, and preparation of food sits with the restaurant partner.

Orders & payments

Placing an order is an offer to purchase at the price shown at checkout. Payments are handled through our payment partners — see the Privacy Policy below for how payment information is handled. The order confirmation and pricing shown in-app at the time of purchase govern the transaction.

Delivery

Estimated delivery times are just that — estimates — and can vary with distance, weather, restaurant load, and traffic. We work with our restaurant and delivery partners to get your order to you as promptly as reasonably possible.

Cancellations & refunds

See our full Refund & Cancellation Policy below for how cancellations, missing items, and refunds are handled.

Prohibited use

Don't misuse Nearbite — this includes placing fraudulent orders, abusing promotions or cash-on-delivery, harassing restaurant or delivery staff, or attempting to interfere with the platform's normal operation.

Account suspension

We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these Terms or misuse the platform, with notice where reasonably possible.

Nearbite's role

Nearbite operates the ordering platform connecting you with independent restaurant and delivery partners. We are not the seller of the food and are not a party to the sale between you and the restaurant, though we help facilitate and support that transaction.

Changes to these Terms

We may update these Terms as Nearbite evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about these Terms? Reach us through Help & Support.

Privacy Policy

What we collect and how it's used

Information we collect

  • Your name and phone number, used to verify your account
  • Delivery addresses and location you provide, used to deliver your orders
  • Order details — items, restaurant, order value, and order history

We only collect what's needed to run your account and fulfill your orders.

How we use it

  • To create and verify your account, and to process and deliver your orders
  • To provide customer support when you contact us
  • To process payments through our payment partners

Who we share it with

We share only what's necessary with the restaurant preparing your order and the rider delivering it (such as your address and order details), and with our payment gateway partner to process payment. We don't sell your personal data to third parties.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. Payment details are processed by our payment gateway partners and are not stored on Nearbite's own servers.

Your rights & account deletion

You can update your name and delivery addresses anytime from your Profile. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data — including closing your account — contact us through Help & Support.

Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted here, with the "Last updated" date above reflecting the most recent revision.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellations and refunds work

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge within 60 seconds of placing it. Once the restaurant accepts the order and begins preparation, it can no longer be cancelled through the app.

Restaurant-cancelled or incorrect orders

If a restaurant cancels your order, or if items arrive missing or incorrect, report it through Help & Support as soon as possible so we can review and process a refund where applicable.

Refund timing

Once a refund is approved, it's issued to your original payment method. Exactly how long it takes to reflect depends on your bank or payment provider and is outside Nearbite's direct control.

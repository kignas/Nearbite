Eatswada restaurant partners

Partner rules & joining requirements

Please review how restaurant onboarding works before applying. This page explains the current application and operating expectations; final commercial terms will be shared by the Eatswada team before onboarding is completed.

1. Who can apply

  • Restaurant owners and authorized representatives of restaurants or cloud kitchens may apply.
  • You must have authority to submit business information and communicate with Eatswada on behalf of the business.
  • The business must be able to serve customers within Eatswada’s currently supported delivery area. Service coverage is confirmed by the team during review.
  • Applications must contain accurate, complete and current information.

2. Information required to apply

  • Owner or authorized contact name and restaurant/kitchen name.
  • A separate business phone number and email address. These must differ from the customer account contact details and must not already belong to another Vendor account.
  • Business type, cuisine, address, map location, opening hours and delivery preference.
  • A valid FSSAI registration/licence number applicable to the food business.

Do not enter a Vendor password in the application. Your customer account is used only to submit the application and check its status.

3. Application review and approval

  1. Submit the application while signed in to your customer account.
  2. Eatswada reviews the submitted details and may request corrections or additional information.
  3. Our team contacts the applicant using the business email and phone number supplied in the form.
  4. Approval of an application is not, by itself, Vendor dashboard access or a guarantee that onboarding is complete.
  5. After contact and verification, the Eatswada team will arrange a separate Vendor account setup process.

4. Separate customer and Vendor accounts

  • The customer account remains a customer account. It can be used to apply and view application status only.
  • Vendor dashboard access uses a separate account and secure sign-in details.
  • Business contact details must be verified before Vendor account activation. A contact already registered to another Vendor account cannot be reused without resolving ownership with Eatswada.
  • Never share passwords or one-time verification codes. Contact Eatswada if you believe an account is being used without permission.

5. Restaurant responsibilities after onboarding

  • Provide accurate menu names, descriptions, prices, photos and availability when the menu is set up.
  • Keep business hours, temporary closures, item availability and preparation information updated.
  • Prepare orders hygienically and safely, and comply with applicable food-safety and business requirements.
  • Accept and prepare orders responsibly, communicate delays or unavailable items promptly, and pack orders suitably.
  • Follow the delivery method agreed during onboarding: self-delivery or Eatswada rider delivery, where available.
  • Handle customer interactions professionally and cooperate with Eatswada on order issues, cancellations and valid complaints.

6. Commercial terms, fees and settlements

Any commission, delivery responsibility, applicable charges, settlement cycle, payment process, promotional participation and other commercial terms will be explained by Eatswada and recorded separately before the restaurant is onboarded. This page does not promise a particular commission rate, fee, order volume or earnings.

7. Changes, suspension and account concerns

Keep the information supplied to Eatswada current. Tell the team if ownership, business contact details, operating address, licence status or delivery arrangements change. Any action affecting a live Vendor account will be handled under the applicable onboarding agreement and platform processes.

8. Privacy and use of application information

Application details are used to review the business, contact the applicant and complete onboarding. Do not submit another person’s private information unless you are authorized to provide it. The application is not a public listing.

Important: This is an onboarding guide, not the final Vendor agreement or legal advice. If any term is unclear, ask the Eatswada team before accepting onboarding or starting to receive orders.

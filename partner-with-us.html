Partner With Us

Last updated on September 29, 2026

For restaurant partners

Bring your food to more people in Maynaguri.

Eatswada connects customers in Maynaguri and nearby serviceable areas with local restaurants. If you run a restaurant, café, bakery, or food business, get in touch to discuss listing your menu on Eatswada.

Why partner with Eatswada?

  • Reach local customers — make your menu discoverable to people browsing Eatswada in our service area.
  • Showcase your menu — present your food items and prices in one place.
  • Local-first platform — Eatswada is focused on Maynaguri and nearby areas.

Listing, commission, order handling, delivery arrangements, and other terms will be discussed with you before onboarding. Contact us for the current details.

How partnership works

  1. Send us an enquiryShare your restaurant name, location, contact details, and a short introduction.
  2. Discuss the detailsWe’ll connect with you to understand your menu, service area, operating hours, and onboarding requirements.
  3. Review and onboardingIf both sides agree on the terms and required details, we can proceed with the restaurant setup.
  4. Prepare your listingReview your restaurant information, menu, item prices, and availability before your listing goes live.

What to prepare

To help us review your enquiry, please have the following information ready where applicable:

  • Restaurant or business name and full address
  • Owner or authorised contact name, phone number, and email
  • Menu with item names and prices
  • Restaurant timings and approximate delivery coverage
  • Applicable business and food-safety details, including FSSAI information

Please do not email passwords, payment PINs, OTPs, or other confidential credentials.

Interested in partnering?

Tell us a little about your restaurant. We’ll review your enquiry and get back to you.

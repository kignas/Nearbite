Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about ordering with Eatswada. The options available for your order will be shown on the website before you confirm it.

Ordering

How do I place an order?

Choose a delivery location, browse available restaurants, add items to your cart, and follow the checkout steps. Review your address and final bill before confirming payment.

Why can’t I see a restaurant?

A restaurant may not appear if it is outside your service area, temporarily unavailable, or not accepting orders. Availability can change during the day.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Changes may not be possible after a restaurant starts preparing your order. Contact support as soon as possible and include your order ID. Any change depends on the order status and restaurant confirmation.

Delivery

Where does Eatswada deliver?

Eatswada is focused on Maynaguri and nearby areas. Delivery depends on your exact address and the restaurant’s available coverage. Check your address on the website before ordering.

See Delivery Areas & Charges for more information.

How much is the delivery charge?

Any applicable delivery charge is shown in your bill before payment. The amount may vary depending on the restaurant, delivery distance, order conditions, or an available offer.

How long will my order take?

Where available, an estimated delivery time is shown on the website. It is an estimate and may change due to preparation time, weather, traffic, or other operating conditions.

Payments

Which payment methods can I use?

Use the payment methods displayed at checkout for your order. Available methods may change, so the checkout page is the source of truth before you pay.

My payment was deducted, but I can’t see an order. What should I do?

Do not immediately pay again. Check your order history and payment app first. If the order is still missing, contact support with the approximate payment time, amount, and transaction reference. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, password, CVV, or full card number.

Order issues & refunds

What should I do if an item is missing or incorrect?

Open Order Help & Issue Reporting and describe the issue. Include your order ID and clear details so the support team can review it.

Can I cancel my order?

Cancellation availability depends on the order status and applicable policy. Contact support promptly if you need help. Please review the Refund Policy for the applicable terms.

When will I receive a refund?

Refund eligibility and processing depend on the reason for the request, the order, and the payment method. Please refer to the Refund Policy and contact support with your order ID if you need an update.

Account & support

How do I update my profile or delivery address?

Open your profile on Eatswada and use the available account or address options. Check that your phone number, address, and landmark are correct before placing an order.

How can I contact Eatswada?

Visit the Help & Support page for the available support options. For an order-related question, keep your order ID ready.

Still need help?

Visit our support page or use the dedicated order-help page if something went wrong with an order.

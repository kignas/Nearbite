Refund & Cancellation

Eatswada Refund & Cancellation Policy

Operated by Kingas Yor · Maynaguri, West Bengal, India
Contact: support@eatswada.com

1. Customer cancellation

You may cancel an order while the order is still marked cancellable in Eatswada. Cancellation availability can change as the restaurant prepares the order. Once an order reaches a non-cancellable stage, the cancellation option may no longer be available.

2. Restaurant cancellation

A restaurant may cancel an order when it cannot fulfil it, for example because an item is unavailable or the restaurant cannot accept the order. If a restaurant cancels an order, Eatswada will not treat the customer as having cancelled it.

3. Missing, incorrect or damaged items

If your order is missing an item, contains the wrong item, or arrives with a material problem, contact Eatswada Support as soon as reasonably possible after delivery. We may ask for order details or supporting information so the issue can be reviewed.

4. Delivery problems

If delivery cannot be completed because the customer provided an incorrect or incomplete address, cannot be reached, or is unavailable at the delivery location, a refund may not be available. We review exceptional circumstances case by case.

5. Refund decisions

Where a refund is approved, the amount and method depend on the circumstances of the order and the payment method used. Eatswada may provide a full refund, partial refund, replacement, or another appropriate resolution depending on the issue.

Because the current Eatswada ordering system uses Cash on Delivery, there may be no online payment amount to return for a COD order. If an online payment method is introduced later, this policy will be updated to explain the applicable refund route and timing.

6. Refund timing

For any approved refund that requires processing through a payment provider, the time for the amount to reach you can depend on the provider or bank. Eatswada cannot guarantee a specific banking settlement time.

7. Fraud and misuse

Eatswada may investigate repeated, abusive, fraudulent, or misleading refund claims. We may restrict refund assistance or account access where there is reasonable evidence of misuse, subject to applicable law.

8. How to request help

Use Help & Support or contact support@eatswada.com. Include your order ID, the problem, and any useful evidence. Requests are reviewed based on the order records and circumstances.

9. Relationship with the Terms

This policy forms part of the Eatswada Terms of Service. If the policy changes, the latest version published on Eatswada will apply to future orders, subject to applicable law.

Need help with an order?

Contact Eatswada Support and include your order ID.

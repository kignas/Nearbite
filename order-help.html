Order Help & Issue Reporting

Last updated on September 29, 2026

Customer support

Something not right with your order?

We’re here to help with order, item, delivery, and payment concerns. Choose the issue below to prepare an email to Eatswada support. Include your order details so we can understand what happened.

What do you need help with?

Select the closest option. This opens your email app with a subject line ready; you can add the details and send it to our support team.

Missing item Wrong item received Damaged or poor-quality item Delivery problem or delay Payment deducted / payment problem Something else about my order

What information should I include?

  1. Order IDFind it in the order details in your Eatswada account, if an order was placed.
  2. What happenedDescribe the issue clearly and tell us which item or part of the delivery is affected.
  3. Useful evidenceIf relevant, attach a clear photo or payment reference. Do not include confidential payment information.
Never share your password, OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, full card number, or payment login details with anyone, including Eatswada support.

Contact Eatswada support

If you’re unable to use the issue options above, email us directly. Please include your registered email or phone number so we can identify the account, and never include sensitive credentials.

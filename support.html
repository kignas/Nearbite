Grievance & complaints

Eatswada is operated by Kingas Yor. For order complaints, refunds, privacy/data requests, account issues, restaurant concerns, or other grievances, contact us using the details below.

Email: support@eatswada.com

For personal-data access, correction, nominee, grievance, or account-deletion requests, use Privacy & Data. You may be asked to verify that you control the account.

Please include your order ID (if applicable), registered phone number/email, and a clear description of the issue. Do not send passwords, OTPs, card numbers, CVVs, or other sensitive authentication/payment credentials.

Frequently asked questions

Orders Payments Delivery Account Refunds & cancellations

Orders

How do I track my order?

Open My Orders from your profile to see live status — from order placed to out for delivery.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Once an order is placed, items and quantities can't be edited — you can only cancel it, within the cancellation window below. To change your order, cancel it in time and place a new one.

Why can't I message the restaurant directly?

Eatswada is the ordering platform — restaurant partners prepare and hand off the food. For anything about your order, contact Eatswada support and we'll coordinate with the restaurant on your behalf.

Payments

What payment methods are supported?

This depends on what's enabled at checkout on your account, including cash on delivery where available. The options you can use are always shown on the payment step.

My payment was deducted but the order didn't go through. What now?

This can happen if a payment doesn't reach us in time. Use "Payment problem" above and provide your order ID, payment reference/transaction ID, date, and amount if available. Never send an OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, full card number, password, or payment login credentials.

Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on the restaurant and your distance from it — an estimate is shown before you place your order and updates in real time once it's on the way.

What if my delivery is running late?

Check live tracking in My Orders first. If it's significantly delayed, use "Delivery problem" above to reach our team.

Account

How do I update my name or delivery address?

Go to your Profile to update your name, phone number, and saved addresses at any time.

How do I delete my account?

Open Privacy & Data while signed in to request account deletion. Contact support if you need help or cannot access your account.

Refunds & cancellations

Can I cancel my order?

Yes — free cancellation is available within 60 seconds of placing an order. Once the restaurant accepts and starts preparing it, cancellation is no longer possible.

When will I get my refund?

Approved refunds are sent to your original payment method. Exact timing depends on your bank or payment provider rather than on Eatswada directly. See our full Refund & Cancellation Policy for details.

An item was missing or wrong in my delivery. What do I do?

Use the "Missing item" or "Wrong item" option above right after delivery — the sooner you report it, the faster we can resolve it.

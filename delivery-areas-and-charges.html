Delivery Areas & Charges

Last updated on September 29, 2026

Eatswada delivery

Local food, delivered to your door.

Eatswada is focused on serving customers in Maynaguri and nearby areas. Delivery availability depends on your address, the restaurant, and the delivery coverage available when you place an order.

Maynaguri, West BengalOur current service focus. Nearby locations may be serviceable depending on the address and restaurant.

Check whether we deliver to you

Enter or select your delivery address on Eatswada. The website will check whether the address is within the available delivery area before you proceed with your order.

  • Choose your delivery location carefully, including the locality and a nearby landmark.
  • Delivery availability can differ between restaurants and may change based on operating conditions.
  • If your address is not serviceable, you won’t be able to place an order for that location.

Delivery charges

Any applicable delivery charge will be shown in your order bill before you make payment. Please review the final bill at checkout before confirming your order.

Delivery charges may vary by restaurant, delivery distance, order conditions, or an offer available at the time. Do not rely on an estimate outside the checkout bill.

Delivery time estimates

Estimated delivery times are shown on Eatswada when available. They are estimates, not guaranteed arrival times, and can change due to restaurant preparation, weather, traffic, or other operational conditions.

Please make sure your phone is reachable and your delivery address and instructions are accurate.

Need help with delivery?

If you have a question about delivery availability, a charge, or an ongoing order, contact Eatswada support with your order ID (if applicable).

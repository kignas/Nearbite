Who we are

Eatswada is an online food-ordering and delivery platform that connects people in Maynaguri, West Bengal with local restaurants. You browse partner restaurants and their menus, place an order, and we help get it prepared and delivered to your door.

Eatswada is operated by Kingas Yor from Maynaguri, West Bengal, India. It is currently run as an individual/unregistered business.

How Eatswada works

  1. Browse restaurants & menus. Explore local restaurants near you, with food items and clear prices shown up front.
  2. Add items to your cart. Pick what you want and review the item total, delivery fee, and any savings before checkout.
  3. Set your delivery address. Choose or add a delivery location. Restaurants can deliver only within their delivery range.
  4. Choose how to pay. Pay online (UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, and cards) through Razorpay, or select Cash on Delivery where it is available.
  5. Place your order. Once payment is confirmed, your order is sent to the restaurant to accept and prepare.
  6. Track & receive. Follow your order status live, and a delivery partner brings it to your doorstep.

Our delivery area

Eatswada currently serves Maynaguri and nearby areas. Each restaurant delivers within about 10 km. If you are outside a restaurant's delivery range, that restaurant will show as unavailable for delivery to your address.

Payments

Online payments are processed securely by our payment partner, Razorpay — including UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, and cards. Cash on Delivery is offered on eligible orders where the restaurant supports it.

For how cancellations and refunds are handled, see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Our role

Eatswada is the ordering and delivery platform. Restaurant partners prepare the food and are responsible for its quality, packaging, and food-safety standards. For anything about your order, our support team will help and coordinate with the restaurant on your behalf.
